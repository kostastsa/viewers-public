The Kalman filter, derived

Notes on the recursive update, with the original derivation and a worked example.

Prediction step

At each step the filter propagates the mean and covariance through the linear dynamics, then corrects them with the measurement. The correction is weighted by the gain K, which is large when the prior is uncertainlarge when the prior is more uncertain than the sensor.

Pk|k−1 = F Pk−1 Fᵀ + Q
The gain is just a ratio of uncertainties; everything else is bookkeeping.
def update(x, P, z, H, R):
    S = H @ P @ H.T + R
    K = P @ H.T @ np.linalg.inv(S)
    return x + K @ (z - H @ x), (I - K @ H) @ P
