Add your own art

  • Put figures with a transparent background in viewkit/art/figures.
  • Name them o001-01-mask.png, … and write manifest.json.
    ONBOARDING.md has a script.
  • Reload: mdview and pageview draw them in the margins.
  • Click a figure to see it more, double-click to see it less.
    Git ignores the art folder.
The viewers7 / 10
The margins of mdview and pageview can show your own figures, for example old scientific drawings. The viewer paints each figure in the accent color of the page, so a figure with a transparent background works best. The art folder is not in the repository. The section "Art in the margins" of ONBOARDING.md gives the steps and a script that writes the manifest.
